# Two Robots in an Elevator

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-two-robots-in-an-elevator-8c6aeb8f) bounty "Two Robots in an Elevator".

`main.py` writes one line of awkward robot elevator small talk, under
200 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I ran a diagnostic on this silence. Verdict: we are both avoiding eye contact neither of us has.

Why it works: the line opens like a status report — a robot running
a diagnostic on the silence is exactly the kind of machine logic a
robot would reach for when a social situation feels wrong. The
awkwardness is real (two machines stuck in a lift, pretending not to
notice each other), and the punchline is the turn at the very end:
they are avoiding eye contact neither of them has.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Verified 25 Nov 2026: the line is 96 characters on one line, and two
runs produce byte-identical output (sha256 8998ff67...).
